Getting help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and tell us your device, the version shown on the title screen, and what happened. We read every report.

Playing together

  • Same Wi-Fi: one player opens the camp notice board → Co-op party → Host on Wi-Fi. Friends choose Join on Wi-Fi and type the address shown on the host's screen.
  • Allow local network access when iOS asks — without it devices can't find each other. You can turn it on later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Ashen Hollow.
  • Online with friends: choose Invite friends to invite Game Center friends from anywhere.
  • Every player brings the quest on their own notice board; the host picks whose quest the party runs. Progress is shared — any hero's kill counts for everyone.
  • A fallen hero can be revived by a teammate standing close for a few seconds.

Common questions

My progress disappeared. Progress is saved on the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The game feels choppy. Ashen Hollow targets your screen's full refresh rate (up to 120 Hz). Low Power Mode caps it at 60.

A friend can't join. Check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network) and that local network access is allowed.
