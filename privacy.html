ASHEN HOLLOW

Privacy Policy · effective 31 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Ashen Hollow does not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts to create, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, heroes, camp upgrades, banked embers and quest progress are saved on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. They are never uploaded by the game. Deleting the app deletes them.

Co-op play

  • Wi-Fi co-op connects devices directly on your local network. While you play together, the game sends gameplay information (hero choice, movement, actions, the shared quest and your displayed name) only to the other devices in your party. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.
  • Online co-op uses Apple's Game Center. When you choose to play online, Apple handles sign-in, invitations and matchmaking under Apple's Privacy Policy; the game sees only your Game Center display name and player identifier so it can show your name to your party.
  • iOS asks for local network access because Wi-Fi co-op needs it. The game uses it for nothing else.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it is suitable for players of all ages under COPPA and GDPR-K. It contains fantasy combat against monsters.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
